About me

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the SMARTbiomed Pioneer Centre and the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford, where I am supervised by Peter Visscher and Pier Palamara.

My research focuses on the development of statistical methodology motivated by problems in biology and genetics. I am particularly interested in Bayesian methods and in developing statistical models that can address complex biological questions.

Before joining Oxford, I completed my PhD at Politecnico di Torino (2021–2024), under the supervision of Enrico Bibbona and Gianluca Mastrantonio. During my PhD, I worked on methodological problems in applied statistics across different applications. In particular, I developed a Bayesian estimation model for RNA velocity and a frequentist testing procedure for high-dimensional semicontinuous data.

More broadly, I am interested in applied statistics and statistical methodology, with a particular focus on developing methods that are motivated by and applicable to real-world scientific problems.
