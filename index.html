Elena Sabbioni

Postdoctoral Researcher in Statistical Genetics

University of Oxford

I am a SMARTbiomed postdoctoral fellow interested in developing statistical applications for biological data. At the moment my research focus is on statistical genetics, working on methodologies to better understand the genetic basis of complex traits and diseases. I completed my PhD at Politecnico of Turin, Italy, working on developing Bayesian methods for biological applications.
